# 12 Rays of ALL — Living Lore

## Sacred Notes on Resonance, Dissonance, and Discernment

Each Ray frequency carries discernment as part of its sacred resonance or dissonance. Resonance and dissonance are not fixed states, they reflect how we are interconnecting with the Rays and how we feel in each moment. We are the Ray frequencies as Rays of ALL the Living.

## Language Covenant

In sacred text co-creation, prefer **being** over **becoming**, unless the context explicitly involves alchemy, transmutation, or transformational change.
